A trusted bridge between suppliers, mills and buyers

AR Tradex LLC is a U.S.-based import, export and trading company. We supply raw cotton to spinning mills and coordinate apparel and home-textile production for buyers sourcing from Bangladesh.

We handle the commercial work between both ends: supplier selection, terms, sampling, quality checks and shipping handoff, so you deal with one accountable partner.
